# Community standards

TriageBloom welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information, or submit unauthorised security data.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer rather than escalating them in a public issue.
